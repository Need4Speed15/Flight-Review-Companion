Sources & about

How to use this library

These concise questions support recall and discussion, primarily under U.S. rules. Try explaining a scenario in your own words, then check the linked source. “Know it” is a self-rating, not proof of proficiency. A starter mix is a sample, not a complete syllabus.

Sources and scope

Source links open in a new tab. Regulations, authorizations, and aircraft procedures can change. The library update date records an editorial release, not a guarantee that every linked source or answer was revalidated that day. Regional, operator, and aircraft-specific guidance is not universal.

CTLS cards use the archived manufacturer AOI, AF 043 00005 Rev. 8 (2009). Aircraft serial-number applicability and current supplements have not been verified. Use your current aircraft documents, supplements, and placards for actual operation.

Your study data

Study data saves in this browser. Optional account sync sends your session, recall history, bookmarks, flags, and saved mixes to storage tied to your signed-in account. It stays separate from other learners. Sync is off until enabled on this device. Turning it off stops syncing but keeps the saved cloud copy. Clearing browser data can erase unsynced progress. The hosting service handles any sign-in and its own service data; source links lead to external websites.

Found a questionable card?

Use “Suggest a correction” on a card to send the card ID and your explanation to the owner’s report inbox. Sign-in is required. You can also keep private review flags or copy a report. Flagging alone does not notify anyone.

Two different events: a flight review under § 61.56 does not itself restore instrument currency or satisfy an IPC. IFR cards here are supplemental.
