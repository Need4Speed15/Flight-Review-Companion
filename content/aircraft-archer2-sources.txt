Scope and limitations
This is a revised archived copy, not an unamended first edition. The title
identifies serial 28-8390087, registration OE-KBS. The publisher's warning
limits operational use to the applicable airplane and requires current
revisions. Its core pages carry different revision dates. These cards are
model/edition-specific study material, not proof that any other PA-28-181
has identical approved limits, instruments, equipment, or procedures.

The archive also contains aircraft-specific weight-and-balance changes and
later fuel, Garmin G5, GNS 430W, and GFC 500 supplements. Those approvals,
STCs, and that airplane's actual empty weight were not generalized into the
deck. No Archer III, Archer TX, diesel conversion, or fuel-injected procedure
was substituted for this carbureted Archer II basic handbook.

Sections checked directly in the handbook
- §1.1: operational applicability and pilot responsibilities.
- §§1.3–1.7: engine, fixed-pitch propeller, and listed aviation fuel.
- §§2.3–2.21: airspeeds, indicator arcs, weights, CG applicability,
  maneuver restrictions, operating approval, and usable fuel.
- §§3.9–3.31: takeoff/in-flight power loss, glide, fire distinction,
  oil/fuel pressure, temperature, electrical failure, open door, carburetor
  icing/roughness. Emergency cards summarize recognition and priorities;
  they do not reproduce a full emergency checklist.
- §§4.19–4.33: ground check, takeoff, climb, cruise, approach/landing,
  and shutdown.
- §5.3 and §5.7 Figure 5-35: unfactored chart assumptions and paved,
  level, dry landing runway. Figure 5-35 was also rendered for visual
  verification of the chart's stated conditions and printed page 5-28.
- §6.1: current individual-aircraft empty weight/CG and loading.
- §§7.7–7.29: gear, brakes, controls, fuel, electrics, vacuum/instrument
  power, pitot-static, cabin heat, restraint, baggage, and stall warning.

Important numerical facts checked against the PDF
- VNE 154 KIAS; VNO 125 KIAS; VFE 102 KIAS.
- VA 113 KIAS at 2,550 lb and 89 KIAS at 1,634 lb; interpolate linearly.
- White arc 49–102 KIAS.
- Normal maximum ramp/flight weight 2,558/2,550 lb; utility maximum
  flight weight 2,130 lb. Baggage 200 lb normal / zero utility.
- Fuel 50 US gallons total / 48 usable; 24 usable per tank; bottom of
  filler-neck tab indicates 17 usable gallons per tank.
- Basic electrical system 14 V / 60 A alternator and 12 V battery.
- Manual flap positions 10, 25, 40 degrees.
- Magneto run-up 2,000 RPM; maximum drop 175 RPM; maximum differential
  50 RPM; no more than 10 seconds on one magneto.
- Gross-weight climb speeds: best rate 76 KIAS / best angle 64 KIAS;
  recommended en route climb 87 KIAS.
- Normal rotation range 48–53 KIAS varies with weight. Short/soft-field
  procedure uses 25-degree flaps; full procedure remains necessary.
- Approach about 75 KIAS initial / 66 KIAS final with flaps extended.
- Power-off best-glide entry 76 KIAS, optional air conditioner off.

Ambiguities deliberately avoided
This copy's §§1.3/2.7 distinguish a five-minute 180-BHP/2,700-RPM takeoff
rating and a 178-BHP/2,650-RPM continuous rating. Those values differ from
many generic Archer summaries, so no unqualified continuous-RPM card was
created. Oil-pressure maximum markings also vary (90 or 100 psi); no
universal maximum was asserted. No universal battery endurance, crosswind
limit, empty weight, fuel-STC approval, or avionics capability was invented.
