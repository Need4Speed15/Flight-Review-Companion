## Scope and result

Revised 42 cards (41 question/answer revisions and one source-key correction). Kept every card ID and category count. No exact normalized question duplicates were found. This is an editorial and reference review, not an FAA approval or a guarantee that every statement has been independently validated against every applicable aircraft configuration. Current regulations and aircraft documents control.

## Sources and verification limits

- [Current Part 61](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-61) and [Part 91](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-91): the eCFR pages consulted reported currency through October 26, 2026. Particular checks included §§ 61.56, 91.169, 91.175, 91.185, 91.413, and 91.417.
- [FAA AIM](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/): departure restrictions, holding, approaches, weather reporting, and aeromedical guidance.
- [PHAK](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/phak), [AC 61-98E](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_61-98E.pdf), and [FAA MOSAIC information](https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/MOSAIC): supporting reference checks. No blanket claim that sport pilots are prohibited from all night operations was introduced.
- The FAA lists [Aviation Weather Handbook 28B](https://www.faa.gov/regulationspolicies/handbooksmanuals/aviation/faa-h-8083-28b-aviation-weather-handbook). Its full PDF exceeded the web reader's size limit; the weather review also used the available AIM weather material. A fresh line-by-line comparison of all weather answers to that entire handbook was not completed.
- [Manufacturer-hosted CTLS AOI AF 043 00005 Rev. 8](https://flightdesignusa.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/AF04300005_08.pdf): publicly available archived copy of the same 107-page document identified by the database. It contains internal unit discrepancies. Aircraft-specific cards now link to this archived source. This review did not re-access the private ForeFlight document or validate serial-number applicability, current supplements, installed engine/propeller/parachute, placards, or weighing record. Those checks remain necessary before relying on numerical aircraft limits or emergency procedures.
